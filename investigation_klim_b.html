Intervention description

p. 2: The high ability treatment called the ‘vanguard’ program consisted, with some variation among schools, of separate instruction in reading, language, mathematics, and science while students were mixed in physical education, home room, recess, music, and art.

p. 1: Ability grouping is the method of assigning students to separate classrooms so that children of a particular age and grade who function similarly in learning achievement and capability are placed together for instruction.

Duration: 36 months
